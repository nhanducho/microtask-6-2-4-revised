Key takeaway

One disruption. Different outcomes.

A supplier bankruptcy may threaten both competitors, but it does not affect every company—or every function—in the same way.

What makes the difference?

Preparedness and response: available inventory, qualified alternative suppliers, operational flexibility, financial resources, and the speed at which teams act together. A competitive opportunity exists only if the company can respond better than its rival.
